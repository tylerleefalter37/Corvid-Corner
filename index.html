📖 THE KEEPER'S MANUAL

1. Mendelian Genetics & Plumage

Plumage follows real Mendelian inheritance. Recessive morphs include Albino (ruby-eyed), Leucistic (white plumage, dark eye), Slate Smoke, Sepia Fawn, and ultra-rare Chimera Eclipse. Codominant traits (Crests, Hooked Beaks, Oil-Slick) upgrade into Super forms when homozygous.

2. Curio Cabinet & Molt Alchemy

Every day, your corvids have a chance to undergo a molt and drop pristine shed feathers corresponding to their visual genetics. Pin these feathers in your Curio Cabinet for permanent global syndicate buffs, or brew them into Gene Tonics to intentionally boost your next breeding clutch.

3. Stray Roost Liquidation

The Stray Roost features a 3-row by 3-lane revolving inventory that restocks automatically. Corvids here are discounted by 70% to 90% due to flight injuries or unknown origins—providing an affordable way to discover carrier genes (hets).

4. Retro Stat Sliders & IVs

Each bird inherits 5 core stats rated 1 to 100. High Cunning enhances lockpicking and counter-pecks; Mandible Force deals direct beak-crushing damage; Flight Velocity governs initiative and dodging.
